ClearDepth

Water safety for boaters. ClearDepth shows you community-reported hazards on lakes across the US, adjusts their severity based on real-time water levels, and alerts you when you're approaching something dangerous.

  • Community hazard reporting on 150+ lakes
  • Live USGS water level data
  • Proximity alerts near flagged hazards
  • Per-lake community chat
